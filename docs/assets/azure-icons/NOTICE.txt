The original Microsoft_Terms_of_Use.pdf is included without modification.
manifest.json records the exact archive entries and SHA-256 digests.

SVG files retain their original bytes. PNG files are transparent proportional
renders for PowerPoint compatibility. No icon artwork is cropped, flipped,
rotated, recolored or distorted. Product names remain adjacent to service icons.
The general Browser and Toolbox symbols are capability symbols, not logos for
the portal, WebIQ or a new Azure service.
